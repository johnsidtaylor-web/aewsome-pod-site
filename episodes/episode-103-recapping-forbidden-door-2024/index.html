Episode 103 - Recapping Forbidden Door 2024

Show notes

In Episode 103 of The AEW-some Pod, pillars Diana Price (Darcy the Mail Girl from Shudder's Last Drive-in with Joe Bob Briggs) and comedian Johnny Taylor (The Hard Times, Doug Loves Movies) are joined by guest host Gringo Fantastico for an in-depth discussion on AEW's Forbidden Door PPV. The trio delves into several key moments from the event, including: - Chris Jericho's impressive performance with the Learning Tree gimmick despite a trios match loss. - The crowd's underwhelming reaction to Orange Cassidy contrasted with their enthusiasm for Zack Sabre Jr. - Toni Storm's victory and the surprising follow-up with a three-way kiss. - The unforgettable main event between Swerve Strickland and Will Ospreay, which left Diana with only faint memories despite being ringside.
