🎙️ Welcome to the AEW-some Pod: Your Ringside Pass to All Elite Wrestling! Get ready to kick off the new year with Diana Prince, Brendan Petrizzo, Mistress Harley, and Johnny Taylor as they dive headfirst into the electrifying world of AEW! In this episode, the crew dissects the thrilling finals of the CC showdown between Moxley and Kingston. Is Kingston’s shot at victory a real possibility or just wishful thinking? And the million-dollar question - who's the real devil lurking in AEW? Is it the cunning Adam Cole, the master manipulator MJF, or could there be a wildcard in the mix? (Sorry, Samoa Joe, you might not fit the bill this time!) Tune in as the AEW-some Pod crew serves up lively debates, insider insights, and unrivaled enthusiasm for all things All Elite Wrestling.
