Show notes

This week on an actual episode of the AEW-some pod, Diana Prince (Shudder's The Last Drive-In with Joe Bob Briggs), Johnny Taylor (Bummin' with the Devil on Tubi), and Gringo Fantastico (Fantastico Disasterpiece Theatre on Troma Now) break down AEW Collision from Atlantic City!
