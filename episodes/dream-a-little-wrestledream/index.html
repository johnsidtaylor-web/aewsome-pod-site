Episode 204 has Diana Prince (Darcy the Mail Girl from Shudder’s Last Drive-In), Johnny Taylor (Bummin’ With the Devil on Tubi, The Hard Times), and Gringo Fantastico (Troma’s Fantastico Disasterpiece Theater) bringing you ringside for AEW’s massive 3-hour Dynamite/Collision super show!
