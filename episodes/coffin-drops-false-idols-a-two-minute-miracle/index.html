Show notes

On Episode 226 of The AEW-some Pod, Diana Prince (The Last Drive-In with Joe Bob Briggs, Halloween Ends), Johnny Taylor (The Hard Times, Stand-Up! Records, Tubi), and Gringo Fantastico (Troma's Disasterpiece Theater) recap AEW Dynasty where MJF outlasted Kenny Omega in a 39-minute classic, Moxley destroyed Ospreay, Kevin Knight won the TNT Title, and Darby earned his world title shot. Then they break down Dynamite from Everett where Darby cashed in that shot the same week and dethroned MJF with four Coffin Drops in under two minutes to become the NEW AEW World Champion. Plus Kenny and Ospreay's emotional backstage moment, Ciampa calling his shot, and Sting's pre-match pep talk.
