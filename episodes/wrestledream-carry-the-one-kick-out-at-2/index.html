Show notes

It's episode 252, WrestleDream 2026 is in the books, and Johnny Taylor, Diana Prince, and Gringo Fantastico are back to break down a night of great wrestling, questionable decisions, and a main event that required an abacus, a flowchart, and a degree in theoretical mathematics. The crew celebrates Nick Wayne's continued rise while explaining why the internet wrestling community's obsession with the dreaded "Jericho Vortex" is about as intelligent as the average wrestling Twitter argument. Mercedes Moné apparently mistakes the post-show media scrum for a chance to bury Thekla, Andrade and Tommaso Ciampa put on an absolute clinic (with an unexpected assist from a pair of pants), and Ilja Dragunov arrives in AEW, bringing his trademark brand of brutally stiff, chest-caving violence to a locker room that might want to invest in some extra ice packs. Plus, Jon Moxley and Mark Davis beat the hell out of each other, the trios titles change hands, and Clockwork Carnage somehow makes filing your taxes look like a relaxing afternoon. Was WrestleDream another home run for AEW, or have we reached the point where the next pay-per-view starts before we've finished watching the last one?
