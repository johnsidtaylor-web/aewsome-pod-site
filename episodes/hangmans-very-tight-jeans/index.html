Show notes

It's time for episode 248! Johnny’s having technical difficulties, so Gringo and Diana are left without adult supervision. Somehow they still manage to review three hours of Dynamite and Collision from Fishers, Indiana. They were actually in the building for a night of bangers, a completely unhinged crowd, Will Ospreay casually ignoring the laws of physics, and Nick Wayne trying to survive a verbal sparring session with Chris Jericho. But none of that answers the episode’s most pressing question: "Just how tight are Hangman's jeans?" Fortunately, Diana conducted some accidental investigative journalism after ending up on the same plane as Hangman—and folks, those pants are apparently fighting for their lives. Add Diana’s eternal war with Claudio, Gringo continuing his completely one-sided vendetta against Gabe Kidd and his alleged black hole of charisma, some Wheeler Yuta appreciation, and Gringo and Diana repeatedly realizing how much they rely on Johnny, and you’ve got another impeccably professional episode of the AEW-Some Pod. Journalism has never sounded this unhinged
